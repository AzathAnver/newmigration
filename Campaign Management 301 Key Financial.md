Campaign Management's Role for Financial Management
Lesson 1 of 7
As we’ve learned in the course "Introduction to Campaign Management", our campaign management system Campaign Management is vital in helping you optimize and manage your campaigns throughout the entire campaign lifecycle. Campaign Management was specifically built to simplify operations, solve for data inconsistency and enforce global best practice governance. 

Campaign Management enables the following advantages for users who juggle many different platforms and inventory across global markets:

1
Operational workflow management (via campaign setup workflow, standardization of naming convention, notifications on spends, etc.)

2
Financial Management, Risk Mitigation & Compliance (via budget management in Olive, monthly spend confirmation & reconciliation, supplier invoice approval, client approval, etc.)

3
Ongoing reporting & advanced analytics (via trafficking workflow, mapping to advertising platforms, data management, etc.)

In this course, we'll focus on the financial management in Campaign Management

Campaign Management is integrated with several financial systems such as SAP and Mediaocean Spectra that regularly sync with Olive to exchange relevant information related to client billing and/or supplier invoices. 

Which finance system Campaign Management is integrated with differs by market. This also means that some tasks in Campaign Management may or may not be required for you to complete, as they are tied to the finance system in use. For example:

If your market uses SAP, you're required to also review and approve supplier invoices through Campaign Management. Please refer to our dedicated training course or the Campaign Management KB(opens in a new tab) for more details.

If your market uses Mediaocean Spectra, you'll have to manually add some additional information to Campaign Management IOs. More on this topic below.

approving supplier invoices
Published 4/1/2025
When a supplier sends an invoice to the agency to charge the agency for the media bought from them, the agency’s Accounts Payable team will scan the invoice and upload it to SAP from where it automatically syncs with Campaign Management. Once the supplier invoice has reached Campaign Management, the Campaign Management IO owner will receive an email notification to approve the invoice so that the agency can pay the supplier the correct amount on time. 

Please note that Campaign Management IO owners will need to provide suppliers with the Campaign Management insertion order ID to add to the invoice and ensure the sync between SAP and Campaign Management is successful.

Approving Invoices via the Media Plan
To approve a supplier invoice from the media plan screen in Campaign Management:

2) Click on the Invoices tab.

6) Select your Resolution to either Approve or Dispute the invoice.


7) If Dispute is chosen, specify the Reason from the drop-down below.


8) Click on Send.

9) Follow up with your regional Accounts Payable team as to why you’re disputing the invoice, and follow up with the supplier to receive an updated invoice.

10) Once an updated invoice has been received, AP will have to credit the invoice out and enter the new one. Invoice owners will then be required to approve:
 

the original invoice
the offsetting credit
the new and revised invoice

Changing the Campaign Management IO Owner
Changing the owner of an Campaign Management insertion order is crucial when the previous owner is no longer responsible for the media buy or has left the business. Campaign Management IO owners are responsible for the following tasks and not updating the owner will create financial havoc:
 

approving supplier invoices
confirming final spend of plan lines
 

In order to change the IO owner:

 

1) Navigate to your media plan and select Draft View.

 

2) Click on the Insertion orders tab.

3) Click on the name of your insertion order to Edit IO Details.

 

4) On the new page that opens, click on Edit.

5) Change the Owner of the IO.

6) Click on Save.

 

7) Click on Publish All Changes on your media plan.

If the previous owner of Campaign Management IOs needs to hand over a large amount of IOs to a new owner, you can also reach out to the Campaign Managament Support team to transfer all IOs to a new owner in bulk.

Making Campaign Management IOs not Cancelable
Campaign Management allows users to indicate when an IO cannot be cancelled via a checkbox on the Campaign Management IO. By default, this checkbox is unticked and displayed as No in the read-only media plan views.

 

To make an Campaign Management IO not cancelable:

 

1) Navigate to your media plan and select Draft View.

 

2) Click on the Insertion orders tab.

3) Click on the name of your insertion order to Edit IO Details.

 

4) On the new page that opens, click on Edit.

5) Untick the checkbox Cancelable.

6) Click on Save.

 

7) Click on Publish All Changes on your media plan.

Manually Creating Campaign Management IOs
While Campaign Management automatically generates Campaign Management insertion orders when users create plan lines, there may be scenarios where you need to manually create an Campaign Management IO. For example to split DV360 adx from non-adx budgets. 

 

To manually create an Campaign Management IO:

 

1) Navigate to your media plan and select Draft View.

 

2) Click on the Insertion orders tab.

3) Click on Add IO.

4) In the new window that opens, complete all fields to provide information about the new IO.

 

5) Click on Save.

 

6) Click on Publish All Changes on your media plan.

Indicating Minor Managed Media Buys
In cases where our agency has minor involvement in managing Google house ads activity, an Campaign Management IO is considered Minor managed and needs to reflect this status to correctly calculate fee amounts.

 

To set a Campaign Management IO to minor managed:

 

1) Navigate to your media plan and select Draft View.

 

2) Click on the Insertion orders tab.

3) Click on the name of your insertion order to Edit IO Details.

 

4) On the new page that opens, click on Edit.

5) In the Management level field, select Minor from the dropdown.

6) Click on Save.

 

7) Click on Publish All Changes on your media plan.

Uploading a Client Purchase Order
Lesson 2 of 7
A client purchase order (client PO) stores administrative information relating to a particular media plan in Campaign Management such as budgets and campaign flight.

Client POs are necessary in order for the agency Finance team to be able to collect payment from the client. They are typically provided to the Client Lead or Planning teams in PDF form and need to be uploaded to Campaign Management before any media activity can go live. We upload them to Campaign Management for the following reasons:

Client invoicing

We need to quote the client PO number on our invoice to the client in order for the agency to get paid by the client.

Avoid overspend
We need to reconcile spend against client PO value to make sure we do not overspend, which would result in the agency not getting paid.

Reduce financial risk
We need to be able to meet all necessary financial, accounting and audit requirements.

Confirming Monthly Spend
Lesson 3 of 7
At the end of the campaign, we need to reconcile the campaign, which means comparing what we have spent on the campaign to what we initially planned to spend (i.e. comparing spend to budget). In order to speed up this process, we confirm spend for each plan line on a monthly basis for the previous month, instead of waiting until the end of the campaign. We call this monthly spend confirmation (MSC). MSC (and campaign reconciliation in general) is important for the following reasons:

Client billing

We need to invoice our clients in line with their commercial agreement

Payment to suppliers

We need to pay our suppliers in time
Reduce financial risk
We need to be able to meet all necessary financial, accounting and audit requirements

Common Monthly Spend Confirmation Scenarios
Lesson 4 of 7
So far we have covered the standard process of confirming monthly spend on both media and service plan lines. This standard process, however, assumes that your confirmed spend equals the delivered spend that Campaign Management tracks for a given month while the campaign is in flight.

Reconciling a Media Plan
Lesson 5 of 7
After a media plan has reached its end date, at midnight (UTC), Campaign Management automatically moves all client-approved media plans that have ended into the Reconciliation stage. This includes media plans that have been pushed back to their published version to correct any errors or update confirmed spend amounts. 

Here's what you need to do BEFORE and AFTER the plan moves into the Reconciliation stage:


Actions to take BEFORE a plan moves into the Reconciliation stage
Ensure the budget on all plan lines is up-to-date.

Check that all plan lines have correct information, but most importantly those fields:

start & end dates

supplier

property

liable entity

currency

cost model


Actions to take ONCE a plan has moved into the Reconciliation stage
After the plan has moved into the Reconciliation stage, here are some tasks you need to complete before the media plan can be reconciled (moved to its last stage):

Confirm monthly spend for the final month on all plan lines and upload proof of delivery. You will see a green tick mark next to your plan lines to indicate that monthly spend confirmation is now complete on those lines across all months. You can see an example in the image below.

Check that your mappings between Campaign Management and activation platforms are correct. Manually upload any stats if needed, as you won't be able to modify data once the plan is locked and moved into the Reconciled stage.

What happens next?

The media plan will be locked

Once spend has been confirmed on all plan lines (you see the green tick marks), Client Finance locks the media plan to complete client invoices. The video on the right shows a demo of the steps that the Finance team takes in Campaign Management to reconcile a media plan.

Once a media plan is locked, no more changes can be made to it. This includes manual stats uploads.


But what if I found a mistake and need to make a change in my media plan?

You'll need to get your media plan unlocked by the Client Finance team!

Reconciliation process: Media plans that reach their end dates will automatically move into the Reconciliation stage. Up to this point, please ensure all information on your plan lines are correct. You will now need to confirm monthly spend for the final month across all plan lines, and you have the last chance to fix any data issues.


Unlocking media plans: Only the Client Finance team has the power to unlock media plans. This is required if you need to update your plan line details, update previously confirmed spend, or to manually upload stats.

financial processes FAQs

Why does my Agency Fee IO not sync to SAP?
As the agency collects revenue from agency fees separately from media spends from their clients, agency fee insertion orders do not sync from Campaign Management to SAP.

My spend is higher than the budget. What do I do?
There can be two cases of spend being higher than the plan line budget:
 

Overspend has NOT been approved by the client: You must confirm that higher total amount in the monthly spend confirmation process. Please follow the instructions in this knowledge base article on how to handle overspend, and asking a senior member of your team to review the overspend.
Overspend HAS been approved by the client: This is ultimately a change in budget which means that the plan line budget will have to be adjusted prior to the end of the flight. You should NOT use the overspend functionality when confirming monthly spend simply because you haven’t updated your budget in a timely manner. You can follow instructions in this knowledge base article on how to edit plan lines and update budgets.
My supplier invoice differs from the confirmed spend. What should I do?
Users can refer to this decision tree for guidance on when they do, and do not, need to change their previously confirmed monthly spend to match a supplier invoice that differs.

How do I get access to edit the Suppliers section in Campaign Management?
Access to update the Suppliers section will only be granted to specific members of the Accounts Payable team who are responsible for updating suppliers in both SAP (our agency's finance system) and Campaign Management as part of their role.

Can I delete previous versions of Terms and Conditions?
No, this is required for audit purposes.

 

When you edit T&Cs, you have the ability to change the section header titles, the content, edit languages and add as many new section headers as you like. You can save changes or save a new version.

 

Different versions will begin to accumulate (Version 1, Version 2, Version 3). Any version can be ‘Set as default’. There is no ability for Media Activation teams to select the version of T&Cs.

I have uploaded the wrong supplier IO. Can I delete it?
No. For audit reasons, previously uploaded supplier IOs cannot be deleted. However, you can upload a more recent copy/new supplier IO to Campaign Management instead.

What entity # should I put on the supplier IO to identify the proper Campaign Management IO?
Please use the Campaign Management IO ID# in place of PO # on the paper contracts with suppliers.

Where can I find the Campaign Management IO ID?
To find the Campaign Management IO ID:
 

Navigate to your Campaign Management media plan and select Published View.
Click on the Insertion orders tab. The Campaign Management IO ID is displayed in the first column of the insertion orders table.

I need to update my plan line budget but my media plan is in Reconciliation.
In Campaign Management, the sum of confirmed spend across plan lines should equal the IO budget amounts. To ensure that this is the case, you must always update your plan line budgets before the relevant month ends or before the media plan automatically moves into Reconciliation mode (i.e. you can’t amend the budget for July 2021 in August 2021). You should NOT use the overspend functionality when confirming monthly spend simply because you haven’t updated your budget in a timely manner.

 

You can edit plan line budgets before the month ends without involving the Campaign Management Support team by following the steps outlined in this knowledge base article. Please note that if the new budget exceeds the breach threshold, previously client-approved media plans need to be reapproved.

 

There may be cases where you need to update budgets after your plan has moved into the Reconciliation stage. For those cases, you may reach out to the Campaign Management Support team for help: 
 

Update your confirmed monthly spends to the new budget amounts.

Ask Campaign Management Support to move the media plan back to its Published stage. Please note, before Support can move the plan to its Published stage, our agency Client Finance team must approve these changes

Update your budgets to the correct new amounts.

Send your media plan for re-approvals.

 

If the media plan has already been locked by Client Finance and moved into the Reconciled stage, please follow these steps:
 

Ask our agency Client Finance team to unlock the media plan and the confirmed spend amounts.

Update your confirmed monthly spends to the new budget amounts.

Ask Campaign Management Support to move the media plan back to its Published stage. Please note, before Support can move the plan to its Published stage, our agency's Client Finance team must approve these changes

Update your budgets to the correct new amounts.

Send your media plan for re-approvals.

If I use automatic fee calculation on my service plan lines, do I have to confirm monthly spend for these plan lines?
No, provided the media plan has been approved by the client, monthly spend for automatically calculated fees from service plan lines will automatically confirm once monthly spend for all media plan lines has been confirmed. 

Do offline media plans require monthly spend confirmation?
Yes, all plan lines other than added value and plan lines with unknown budget have monthly spend confirmation enabled.

I get an error saying I need to attach a client PO. I can't publish my media plan!
Your client requires a client PO on Campaign Management. To resolve, please upload a Client PO into Campaign Management with the three steps outlined in the article on client POs. If you have any issues with this, please submit a ticket to the Campaign Management Support team.

In what currency should I raise the Client PO?
The Client PO has to match the Media Plan currency. You may set different currencies at Plan Line level.

Can a client PO be linked to multiple media plans in Campaign Management?
Yes, a client PO can be linked to multiple media plans. You can also have more than one client PO associated with a media plan, however you cannot link multiple client POs to one Campaign Management IO. You will need to split the Campaign Management IO into multiple IOs in order to associate them with different client POs.

My media plan uses the wrong conversion rates.
Please follow the links to identify, whether Campaign Management uses client rate, trading rate or market rate to convert the monetary amounts in your media plan. If, after reviewing the information in this knowledge base article, you still feel that Campaign Management uses the wrong rate, please submit a ticket to the Campaign Management Support team.

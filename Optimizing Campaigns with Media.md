At its core, Media Optimizations' AI dynamically adjusts elements like bidding, budget allocation, and targeting refinements within your campaigns. This allows you to focus on strategic oversight rather than manual, day-to-day adjustments.

Setting Your Campaigns Up for AI Success: Best Practices for Buying Platforms 

For Media Optimizations to deliver maximum uplift, your campaigns in the Buying Platform should be configured to give the AI optimal flexibility and data. Adhering to these setup best practices is crucial: 


Broad Original Line Item (OLI) Targeting: Design your OLIs with broader targeting. This provides the AI sufficient scope to discover and focus on the most performant subsets. 


Minimum OLI Count: Aim for a minimum of 4 Original Line Items per campaign. This gives the AI distinct areas to learn from and allocate spend across, maximizing its effectiveness (Easiest way to do this is to split by device type e.g., Desktop, Mobile, Tablet, CTV). 


Flexible Budget Allocation: Avoid fixed budgets at the Line Item level. Ensure your campaign budget is flexible enough for Media Optimizations to dynamically shift spend towards better-performing areas. 

Imagine Media Optimizations is a chef. If you tell the chef exactly how much of each ingredient to use for every dish, they can't adapt. But if you give them a total budget for all ingredients and tell them the desired outcome (e.g., 'make the best meal possible'), they can dynamically adjust ingredients to make the most delicious dishes. Flexible budget allocation lets Media Optimizations be that smart chef with your ad spend. 


Sufficient Flight Dates: Campaigns should run for a minimum of 4-6 weeks to allow the AI adequate time to learn, identify patterns, and stabilize performance. 


Realistic Budgets: Ensure campaign budgets are realistic (USD 5K minimum/align to campaign setup best practices) to generate enough quality data for the AI to learn effectively. 


Pacing: Set pacing at 120% (pacing ahead) for optimal flexibility. Settings below 100% can adversely impact performance and result in under-delivery. 

Remember: Media Optimizations is a powerful engine, but like building a house, it needs a strong foundation. Your Buying Platform setup - with broad OLIs, flexible budgets, and realistic settings - is that foundation. A solid start ensures the AI can build the most effective campaign performance.

Understanding Strategy Types: Campaign-Level vs Cross-Platform

Media Optimizations offers three strategic approaches, each designed for different campaign needs.  

While we'll first explore the foundational Campaign-Level Optimization (CLO), it's helpful to know that on the platform's strategy creation screen, you'll see these options laid out visually: Cross-Platform Optimization (CPO) on the left, Campaign-Level Optimization (CLO) in the center, and Tactics on the right. 

Campaign-Level Optimization (CLO): Optimizes a single campaign within one Buying Platform. The connected Buying Platform remains the budget source of truth. This is your foundational strategy. 

Cross-Platform Optimization (CPO): Optimizes performance across multiple campaigns and/or multiple Buying Platforms simultaneously. Media Optimizations becomes the budget source of truth. 

Tactics: A legacy strategy. Primarily supported on TTD.  

The Strategy Creation Process: Key Decisions 

Creating a strategy in Media Optimizations involves a guided wizard. The key is understanding the purpose of each step and the impact of your choices on optimization. As you define your strategy, remember that Media Optimizations leverages unique, purpose-built AI/ML algorithms for each specific optimization goal you select, ensuring its powerful AI is precisely tailored to your primary KPI. 

Best Practice: Always create a new Media Optimizations strategy for each new Insertion Order (IO) or campaign. This ensures clean data, avoids conflicts with previous optimizations, and provides the AI with a fresh, optimized starting point for every new campaign.

2. Budget & Goal Definition (Wizard Step 2):
This step defines the core parameters Media Optimizations will optimize against, enabling the AI to make intelligent decisions. How you input your budget and flight dates depends on your chosen strategy type: 

For Cross-Platform Optimization (CPO): 

Input your total budget and campaign flight dates directly into the Media Optimizations platform. Media Optimizations becomes the budget source of truth. 

For Campaign-Level Optimization (CLO):

3. Strategy Type (Wizard Step 3):

4. Configure - Optimization Settings (Wizard Step 4):
What you'll do: Configure options like Viewability and Pacing to fine-tune the AI's behavior and align with campaign needs. 

Best Practices:  

Viewability: If optimizing for viewability as a secondary goal, set a realistic target. Higher targets increase cost and restrict inventory. 

Pacing: Recommended: Set pacing at 120% (pacing ahead) for optimal flexibility. This allows MO's dynamic pacing logic sufficient headroom to accelerate spend on high-performing areas. Setting below 100% can adversely impact performance and result in under-delivery. 

4a. Intelligent Line Items (ILIs): Powering AI Optimization 

What you'll do: Ensure the Intelligent Line Items toggle is enabled (highly recommended for maximum performance uplift). 

Intelligent Line Items (ILIs) are dynamic copies of your Original Line Items (OLIs) that Media Optimizations automatically creates and manages. They are the core engine of Media Optimizations' AI-driven optimization. 

How ILIs work with Predictive Budget Optimization: ILIs continuously refine targeting, settings, and bid values, allowing the AI to explore and focus on the most performant audience segments within your defined targeting.  

Note: When an ILI identifies a better-performing segment, Media Optimizations' predictive budget optimization will dynamically shift budget towards that ILI, and away from underperforming ones.  

This continuous, data-driven adjustment drives your campaigns towards significantly improved or maximum performance. 

Default & Recommendation:  

Intelligent Line Items are toggled on by default for new strategies.  

Your Original Line Item defines the whole 'territory' of potential audience. Intelligent Line Items are like smart prospectors with metal detectors, constantly scanning that territory to pinpoint the exact 'gold veins' – the most performant audience segments – within it.  

We strongly recommend keeping ILIs active for maximum performance uplift, as disabling them significantly limits Media Optimizations' ability to dynamically optimize targeting, negatively impacting campaign performance. 

 

AI Control vs. Your Control: What Settings Media Optimizations Adjusts 

With Intelligent Line Items enabled, Media Optimizations' AI dynamically controls key campaign aspects to drive performance. It primarily influences: 

Bidding: Adjusts bid values to optimize for your primary KPI. 

Targeting Refinement: Dynamically refines specific targeting parameters within your Original Line Item's scope. 

Predictive Budget Allocation: Shifts budget to better performing Line Items for maximum campaign efficiency. 

Crucially, your Original Line Item (OLI) settings remain the primary source of truth for core targeting, overall campaign budget, and flight dates. The AI operates within these boundaries, amplifying your strategic intent. 

For a comprehensive, platform-specific breakdown of what settings Media Optimizations' AI changes, its limitations, and detailed compatibility, refer to the dedicated 'Intelligent Line Items' section in the Media Optimizations Help Center. 



4b. Budget Management: Budget Allocation Groups
What you'll do:  Create Budget Allocation Groups and define their minimum/maximum spend ranges. This applies daily min/max spend across groups of Line Items/Ad Groups, providing granular budget control. 

Best Practices: While this feature offers control, setting too many fixed or narrow ranges can effectively create rigid "recipes" for your "smart chef" (Media Optimizations). This limits the AI's ability to dynamically shift budget to the best-performing areas, potentially constraining or even negatively impacting overall campaign performance and uplift. For optimal results, prioritize flexible budget allocation, allowing the AI maximum freedom to optimize. 



5. Confirm - Review & Create Strategy (Wizard Step 5):
What you'll do: Review the summary and create your strategy. This final review ensures all settings are correct before Media Optimizations begins optimizing your live campaign in your connected Buying Platform.

Managing Live Strategies: Best Practices & Adjustments

Ceasing Optimization: Detach vs. Delete 



Accessing & Modifying:  

From the dashboard, access live strategies to monitor and adjust. 

Note: Core settings like optimization type cannot be changed once live. Adjustment options (e.g., budget, dates, pacing) vary by strategy type (CLO vs. CPO) and its budget source of truth. Please refer to the help center for more information. 

If you need to stop Media Optimizations from optimizing a specific Buying Platform object or an entire strategy, there are different methods with varying impacts. 

Critical Note on Insights: Once optimization ceases (whether by detaching or deleting), the insights package for that strategy/object is no longer retrievable. Always download insights prior to ceasing optimization. 

Recommended Action: Detach Object/Strategy 

What it does: Removes a specific Buying Platform object (or all objects from a strategy) from Media Optimizations control, ceasing optimization for that object/strategy. 

Benefit: Preserves the strategy's presence in the system, allowing for potential re-attachment in the future. 

Impact: Optimization stops for the object/strategy. The strategy remains in Media Optimizations for troubleshooting (though insights are not retrievable). 

Action to Avoid: Deleting a Strategy 

What it does: Removes the entire strategy from Media Optimizations. 

WARNING: This action cannot be undone. All associated insights and historical data for the strategy will be permanently lost. Deleting a strategy should only be considered as a last resort. 

Data Retention: Media Optimizations adheres to privacy laws and data retention policies. Historical data, including insights, may not be available indefinitely. 

Best Practices for Continuous Optimization: 

Regular Monitoring: Continuously review campaign performance in both Media Optimizations using the monitoring and insights tab, and use that as a cue to check on your Buying Platform as needed. 

Strategic Adjustments: Use insights from Media Optimizations to inform necessary adjustments to your strategy or underlying Buying Platform campaigns, ensuring they consistently adhere to Buying Platform best practices (e.g., sufficient ad sizes, realistic CPMs, balanced targeting) to give Media Optimizations the best foundation for success.

Deep Dive: Understanding "Delivery Cap Reached"

A particularly valuable insight is found within the Optimizations Insights > Budget Allocation & Available Scale visualization. Here, Media Optimizations' predictive budget optimization dynamically allocates more budget to better-performing Line Items. For instance, if a Line Item is excelling, the AI might predict it can spend $70 and proactively allocate a buffer and increasingly higher amounts to maximize its potential. 

If you observe the caution “Delivery Cap Reached”, this indicates that the AI has attempted to spend more on that Line Item but is being constrained. This caution appears when the average delivery is lower than or equal to 70% of the average allocated budget (e.g., if $100 was allocated, but only $70 was spent). 

Action: When you see this caution, it's a strong signal to investigate the underlying restrictions in your Buying Platform. Utilize tools like the trouble shooter and impressions-lost widget to identify common culprits such as: 

1
Overly restrictive frequency caps 

2
Auction loss due to bidding 

3
Limited inventory for specific targeting parameters 

4
Budget constraints from the setup 

Addressing these issues will allow Media Optimizations to fully optimize and drive better performance.

Common Issues & Proactive Checks

Before seeking support, check for these common issues:  


Data Flow: Verify your Buying Platform seat has already been successfully onboarded to the Media Optimizations platform 


Data Sync Issues: If Intelligent Line Items don't appear within 24 hours of strategy activation, or if campaign data isn't syncing as expected: 

Verify ILI Toggle: Check that the Intelligent Line Items toggle is enabled within your Media Optimizations strategy settings. 

Confirm Campaign State: Ensure your campaign in the Buying Platform is in an 'active' or 'live' state. (Crucial for initial sync). 

Review Line Item Format: Ensure your Original Line Items (OLIs) in the Buying Platform adhere to supported formats and best practices 


Pacing Issues: Investigate unusual spikes or drops in delivery. 


Negative Uplift: If performance declines despite optimization. 


Campaign Restrictions: Overly restrictive settings in your Buying Platform (e.g., tight audiences, low CPMs, strict frequency caps) can significantly hinder AI optimization. Always ensure your Buying Platform setup adheres to campaign best practices first to give Media Optimizations the best foundation for success. 


Why it matters: Proactive checks save time and often resolve issues quickly. Understanding common pitfalls helps you set up campaigns for success.

1. What is this project?

This project is a Campaign Management knowledge assistant built using a local RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) architecture.

Its purpose is to allow a user to ask questions such as:

"How do I update a budget when a media plan is in Reconciliation?"
"What should I do if the media plan is Reconciled and locked?"
"Why is MSC not reflecting in SAP?"
"What is Monthly Spend Confirmation?"
"How do I get access to ED 2.0?"
"What should I do when a campaign has ended?"
"How do I troubleshoot a Campaign Management issue?"

Instead of expecting the LLM to know the answers, the system searches the organization's Campaign Management knowledge base, retrieves relevant documents/chunks, and gives those documents to the LLM as context.

22. One-paragraph explanation for another AI/developer

You can literally give this to another AI:

Campaign AI is a local RAG-based Campaign Management knowledge assistant. Campaign Management documentation is ingested from Confluence/Markdown files, chunked, and stored in a SQLite knowledge database with an FTS index and semantic embeddings. At runtime, rag.py accepts a user's Campaign Management question and calls the hybrid retrieval layer in retriever.py, which combines SQLite FTS, keyword scoring, intent scoring, and semantic similarity to retrieve and rank relevant knowledge chunks. Results are deduplicated and converted into a context passed to a local Qwen3:8B model running through Ollama. The system also contains deterministic workflow logic for critical Campaign Management processes, particularly Reconciliation versus Reconciled/locked media-plan workflows, so the LLM cannot arbitrarily reorder documented steps or invent permissions/approvals. Strict answering rules require the model to use only the retrieved knowledge base, preserve documented workflow order, distinguish workflow states, and explicitly say when the documentation does not specify something. The application is an advisory knowledge assistant; it does not directly modify Campaign Management systems or execute the procedures it describes.
